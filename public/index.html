      07 FAQs

      Good questions, honest answers.

      Residential or commercial — does it matter?

      Yes. GST treats a house rented to someone who lives in it differently from a shop, office or warehouse, and the answer can change if the tenant is GST-registered or uses the home for business. KirayaKhata asks simple questions and, where the facts are unclear, says a CA should confirm.

      Does KirayaKhata file GST returns?

      Not today. It prepares the numbers and tells you what to report and by when. You or your CA file on the GST portal. We never ask for your GST portal password.

      Who pays the GST — me or my tenant?

      It depends. A registered landlord renting a shop usually adds GST to the invoice. In some cases the tenant pays GST directly under reverse charge, and you add none. Renting a home to live in is usually exempt. KirayaKhata works out the case or flags it for a CA.

      Why is the money in my bank less than the rent?

      Some tenants deduct TDS (tax) from the rent and deposit it in your name. So your bank receipt is the invoice total minus the TDS. You get credit for that TDS in your tax return, so the amount should match what the tenant reports.

      Can I choose how the invoice looks?

      Yes — three templates (Classic Ledger, Modern Minimal, Professional Letterhead), all with the same figures. You get a live preview and a draft PDF. Every PDF is marked DRAFT / DEMO; verify before issuing.

      Does it send the invoice to my tenant automatically?

      Not in this demo. “Open email draft” opens your own mail app with a summary; you attach the downloaded PDF and send it yourself. A browser page cannot attach a file for you or send on a schedule — that is planned for the full app, with your opt-in.

      Do the calendar dates stay up to date?

      The on-page dates use a fixed rule set with a review date. The calendar file you download is a snapshot at that moment; it will not receive future government extensions automatically.

      Do I still need a CA?

      Yes, for review and filing. KirayaKhata does the routine monthly work — receipts, checking rent and TDS, tracking dates — so your CA gets clean numbers and spends time only where professional judgement is needed.

      Is my information safe?

      We never ask for your PAN, bank details or portal passwords. What our server saves is anonymised, such as the rent amount and property type. Names, GSTINs and emails you type for an invoice stay on your device and are never sent to us.

      Which tax year am I working on, and is it in Hindi?

      Indian tax years run 1 April to 31 March; KirayaKhata works it out from each rent date and labels it plainly, e.g. “Rent earned Apr 2026–Mar 2027”. The whole page works in English and Hindi — use the toggle at the top.
