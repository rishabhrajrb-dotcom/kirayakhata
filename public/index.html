Questions landlords ask

Do I still need a CA?

Yes, for review and filing. KirayaKhata does the routine monthly work — receipts, checking rent and TDS, tracking dates — so your CA gets clean numbers and spends time only where professional judgement is needed.

Does KirayaKhata file GST returns?

Not today. It prepares the numbers and tells you what to report and by when. You or your CA file on the GST portal. We never ask for your GST portal password.

Does KirayaKhata file my income-tax return?

Not today. It keeps a full-year record of rent and TDS and prepares a pack for your CA. Filing stays with you or your CA.

What happens if I miss a deadline?

The item moves to the top of your screen as overdue, with the next step. Late filing or payment can lead to late fees or interest, so speak to your CA promptly. We will not guess the penalty for you.

How does it know what is due next?

It uses a fixed calendar of tax dates, your GST status, whether you file monthly or quarterly, and the rent you have marked. Every reminder says which month or year it is for. Each date rule records its official source and when a CA last checked it.

What is TDS?

Tax deducted at source. Some tenants, such as companies and many businesses, must keep back part of the rent as tax and deposit it with the government in your name. You get credit for it in your tax return, so it matters that the amount matches.

Residential or commercial — does it matter?

Yes. GST treats a house rented to someone who lives in it differently from a shop, office or warehouse, and the answer can change if the tenant is GST-registered or uses the home for business. KirayaKhata asks simple questions and, where the facts are unclear, says a CA should confirm.

Is my tax information safe?

The demo does not ask for your PAN, GSTIN, phone, email, address or bank details, and never for portal passwords. What we save is anonymised, such as the rent amount and property type. Names you type for a receipt stay in your browser.

Which tax year am I currently working on?

Indian tax years run from 1 April to 31 March. You don’t need to remember which one is current: KirayaKhata works it out from the date of each rent payment and labels it plainly, for example “Rent earned Apr 2026–Mar 2027”.
